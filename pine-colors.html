More color: two directions

Both come from the color palettes in the Pine Brand Standards. Each press and department gets one color, used the same way in every app. Shown on three apps: Schedule (desktop), Catching (iPad) and the Pine Central home. These are mockups with made-up jobs; the apps haven't changed.

Same rule in both: Press 1, Press 2, Embroidery and Heat each get one color. You see it on the Schedule rows and cards, Catching's press tabs, and Central's press cards and ticker.
Yellow still means "you": the one main button and "waiting on you" stay Pantone 107, so the new colors never compete with it. The black top bar and logo square stay.
Readable: text on color is black or white, whichever reads best (white only on 7683 and 540). The colors also differ in lightness, so color-blind crew can tell them apart.
