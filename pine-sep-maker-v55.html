    Trap (px)
    House rule at 300 dpi: BB 3 px inside the EZ layer, EZ 3 px inside the colours, lighter colour runs 2 px under the darker one. Stroke + grows every top colour, − shrinks it.
    Lines 6 px (.02″) or thinner normally drop out of the base; 7 px and up keep a thinner one. Tick to keep them all.
